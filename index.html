The usual problems with checking fixtures.

Buyers of checking fixtures for critical assemblies and stampings tell us the same five stories. We built EXCEL to end them.

  1. 01

    Inaccuracy

    The problem

    The fixture fails on CMM in the first attempt, results change from check to check, and pins and bushes have play.

    The EXCEL answer

    Proved out on CMM in one go, with a detailed inspection report for every pin and bush — and no play.

  2. 02

    Delays

    The problem

    Vague timelines, no regular status, endless follow-ups and an eleventh-hour shock.

    The EXCEL answer

    Genuine timeline commitments, authentic regular status updates and early notice of any foreseen delay.

  3. 03

    Ordinary finish

    The problem

    Basic surface finish, weak paint, unprotected areas that rust and confusing labels.

    The EXCEL answer

    Premium aesthetics comparable with international fixtures, special care for rust, coating and paint, and detailed labeling.

  4. 04

    Build quality

    The problem

    Too heavy or too weak, local unbranded parts and quality that changes from fixture to fixture.

    The EXCEL answer

    Standardized child parts, branded bought-out parts only, and the right heat treatment — with proof.

  5. 05

    Slow service

    The problem

    Delayed responses, too many hassles and weak documentation.

    The EXCEL answer

    A responsive team, clear understanding of your needs and complete documentation.
