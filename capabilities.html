Capabilities

Fixtures for critical assemblies & stampings.

We design, build and prove out checking fixtures for automotive sheet-metal assemblies and stampings — from single parts to multi-part master assemblies.

01

Assembly checking fixtures

Fixtures for critical automotive assemblies, designed so every check is repeatable and every result is trusted.

  • Floor, dash and body-side assemblies
  • Axle and suspension beams
  • Steering support members
02

Stamping checking fixtures

Fixtures for critical sheet-metal stampings, built for reliable results on the shop floor.

  • Panels and members
  • Detailed, user-friendly labeling
  • Rust-protected finish
03

Master assembly fixtures

Multi-part master assembly fixtures that bring several mating parts onto a single, CMM-proven base.

  • Multiple parts on one base
  • Proved out on CMM
  • Built to look like a master gauge
04

Design, CMM & support

In-house 3D and 2D design and CMM measurement, with complete documentation and responsive service after delivery.

  • In-house 3D & 2D design
  • CMM and pin & bush reports
  • Responsive after-delivery support
