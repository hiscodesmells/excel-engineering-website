About EXCEL Engineering

Built on peace of mind.

We design and manufacture checking fixtures for critical automotive assemblies and stampings in Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR. Our aim is simple: a fixture your team — and your client — can trust from the first CMM check.

Our story

Trained abroad. Proven in India.

Our foundation is 10 months of on-the-job training in China, followed by 15 years in the Indian checking-fixture industry. Along the way we have worked to the fixture standards of major vehicle OEMs in India — and shaped our process around the problems buyers know too well: CMM failures, delays, rust and endless follow-ups.
